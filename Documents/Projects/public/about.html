ABOUT AEGIS

Pre-disaster intelligence built as a full-stack academic system.

Aegis demonstrates a realistic emergency-management architecture while keeping all risk predictions and operational data clearly simulated.

Detect

Combine environmental inputs with a transparent scoring model to estimate hazard risk.

Prepare

Maintain tasks, shelter options, evacuation plans and emergency resources.

Respond

Coordinate incidents, teams, alerts, notifications and simulated SOS requests.

Safety statement: Aegis is an academic prototype. It does not contact emergency services and must not be used instead of official warnings.
