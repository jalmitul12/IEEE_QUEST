DISASTER AWARENESS

Know the hazard before the siren.

Flood

Move to higher ground, avoid driving through floodwater, and disconnect electricity only when safe.

Earthquake

Drop, Cover and Hold On. Stay away from glass and heavy unsecured objects.

Cyclone

Secure loose items, charge devices, monitor official updates and know your nearest shelter.
